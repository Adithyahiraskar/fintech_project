Privacy policy

Effective date: 1 October 2026

This policy explains what data Bazaarwise ("we", "the service") collects from merchants and their customers, why we collect it, how it is protected, and the choices you have. It applies to the Bazaarwise website, application, and WhatsApp communication service.

1. Data we collect

  • Merchant and business data: name, shop name, contact details, city or area, business category, and supplier information you choose to enter.
  • Product and sales data: product catalog details, prices, stock levels, expiry dates, and records of online and offline sales you record in the service.
  • Customer data you enter: customer name or identifier, phone number (where you collect it with the customer's consent), and purchase history linked to that customer.
  • Early access form data: name, shop name, city, phone number or email, and product category submitted through this website.
  • External signals: weather forecasts, festival calendars, sports fixtures, school calendars, and local event data used to generate recommendations. This is not personal data about you.
  • Technical data: basic logs such as device type and pages accessed, used to keep the service secure and reliable.

2. How we use data

  • To record sales, maintain inventory, and compute stock levels.
  • To generate forecasts, customer segments, and daily recommendations for your shop.
  • To send you the morning brief and alerts through WhatsApp or other channels you enable.
  • To measure recommendation accuracy and improve the service.
  • To contact you about the early access programme if you requested it.

We do not use your sales data to advertise to your customers without your instruction, and we do not sell personal data to third parties.

3. Customer data rules

You confirm that you have a lawful basis, such as the customer's consent, to record their name and phone number in the service. We use customer data only to provide features you have enabled for your own shop, such as retention reminders and targeted offers. Customers may ask you or us to correct or delete their record.

4. Neighbourhood insights

Some recommendations use aggregated demand patterns from a merchant's local area. These insights are anonymised and aggregated across multiple merchants. We apply minimum group sizes before any pattern is shared, so individual transactions cannot be traced to a specific shop or customer. Your raw transaction records are never exposed to another merchant.

5. WhatsApp and messaging

If you enable WhatsApp delivery, your phone number is used to send briefs and alerts you have requested. Message content is limited to your shop's own recommendations. You can disable messaging at any time by replying STOP or by changing your preferences in the service.

6. Data security and retention

  • Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with access controls limited to operations needed to run the service.
  • We keep merchant data while your account is active, and delete or anonymise it within a reasonable period after a verified deletion request, subject to legal requirements.
  • We minimise collection: we do not require personal information that is not needed to provide a feature.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access, correct, or delete data we hold about you or your shop.
  • Export your sales and customer records in a portable format.
  • Opt out of non-essential messages.
  • Ask questions or raise a complaint through the contact details below.

8. Service providers

We use infrastructure and messaging providers (for example cloud hosting and WhatsApp Business delivery) to operate the service. They process data only on our instructions and are bound by contractual confidentiality and security obligations.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we will update this page and, for material changes, notify you through the service or by email before the change takes effect.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or complaints, email privacy@bazaarwise.in. We aim to respond within 7 days of receiving a request.
